**Turn your Anki tags and decks into a visual Mastery Grid and interactive 2D/3D map.** Spot unfinished topics at a glance, search down to your deepest tags and card text, and launch focused Browser searches or cram sessions without touching your scheduling. Completely offline and read-only.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/1-mastery-grid-overview.png" width="640" alt="Mastery Grid overview in Anki colors showing learned vs. suspended topics across subjects">

## What it does

- **See the big picture in Grid view.** Every subject or top-level tag shows a breakdown of mature, young, new, and suspended cards alongside color-coded subtopic blocks, so yellow gaps jump right off the screen.
- **Drill down without getting lost.** Click any block to zoom into its subtopics and open the side Inspector: one click for **Open Gaps**, one click for a non-rescheduling **Cram Deck**, quiet links for weak cards or high-yield gaps, and an expandable **Where missing cards live** breakdown.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/2-drilldown-and-inspector.png" width="640" alt="Drilled-in Cardiology view and calm two-button Topic Inspector">

- **Search deep tags and card text together.** Press `/` and type any keyword to search across topic folders, deep nested tags (Level 4+), and card text at the same time — then jump to the topic on your map or open those exact cards in the Browser.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/3-live-deep-search.png" width="640" alt="Live search matching deep Level 4 tags, topics, and cards simultaneously">

- **Combine multiple tags or subdecks.** Open **Highlight Tags** and check more than one tag or subdeck together to spotlight matching topics across the grid and open combined `OR` queries in the Browser.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/4-multi-tag-highlight.png" width="640" alt="Multi-select Highlight Tags checkbox menu highlighting matching topics">

- **Explore connections in 2D and 3D.** Switch from Grid to **2D** or **3D** to see cards tagged across multiple topics (solid bridges) and local offline text similarity (dashed concept links), isolate any cluster, or trace shortest paths between two topics.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/5-3d-network-connections.png" width="640" alt="3D knowledge network showing cross-topic bridges and connected subjects">

## Works with any deck

- **Nested tags (`::`), flat tags, or subdecks.** Automatically maps your tag tree (languages, computer science, law, sciences, personal study decks) and detects cross-cutting tags as checkboxes in **Highlight Tags**.
- **AnKing Step 1 & Step 2 built in.** Automatically organizes `#AK_Step1_v12` First Aid and `#AK_Step2_v12` Subjects across Step 1, Step 2, or unified All views, with optional resource checkboxes for Pathoma, Sketchy, Boards & Beyond, UWorld, AMBOSS, Bootcamp, and Pixorize.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/6-non-anking-nested-tags.png" width="640" alt="Universal nested-tag support on a non-medical deck with auto-detected tag filters">

## Accessible colors

- **Anki colors + Colorblind mode (`CB`).** Uses Anki's card-state colors (dark green mature, light green young, blue new, yellow suspended) with a one-click Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe mode that updates tiles, graph nodes, inspector indicators, and the bottom legend together.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wilsondong41/anki-atlas/main/listing/7-colorblind-mode.png" width="640" alt="Okabe-Ito colorblind mode updating tiles, dock dots, and bottom legend together">

## Getting started

1. Install Anki Atlas and restart Anki.
2. Click **Atlas** in the top toolbar (or press `Cmd+Shift+M` / `Ctrl+Shift+M`, or open **Tools → Anki Atlas**).
3. Click any block to drill down, check tags under **Highlight Tags**, or press `/` to search cards and deep tags.

## Good to know

- **100% offline.** Zero servers, accounts, CDNs, or external API calls.
- **Strictly read-only on your cards.** Atlas never edits your notes, tags, or review history. **Cram Deck** builds a separate non-rescheduling filtered deck (`resched=False`) and brings Anki's main window forward to study it.
- **Three clean levels on the map, deep tags one click inside.** The overview stays fast and readable at Levels 1–3, while clicking any leaf topic reveals **Specific Tags Inside** (Level 4+) and search hits everything.
