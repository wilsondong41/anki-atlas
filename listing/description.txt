Turn any Anki deck or nested tag tree into an interactive **Mastery Grid** and **2D/3D Knowledge Map** — find learning gaps fast, drill down to exact subtopics and deep tags, and build custom cram decks without touching your scheduling.

**Works with any Anki setup**
- **Nested tags (`::`):** Automatically detects your tag hierarchy (languages, CS, law, sciences, custom study tags) and lets you filter cross-cutting tags alongside them.
- **Subdecks & flat tags:** Falls back cleanly to your deck tree or tag groups when you don't use nested tags.
- **Medical / AnKing support built in:** Automatically maps `#AK_Step1_v12` First Aid & `#AK_Step2_v12` Subjects across Step 1, Step 2, or unified All views, with optional cross-tag highlighting for Pathoma, Sketchy, Boards & Beyond, UWorld, AMBOSS, Bootcamp, and Pixorize.

**What you can do inside Anki Atlas**
- **Mastery Grid + 2D / 3D Map:** Switch between a fast proportional tile grid (click any block to drill down) and interactive 2D/3D topic networks.
- **Multi-select Tag Highlighting:** Check multiple tags or subdecks together under **Highlight Tags** to see matching topics and open combined `OR` searches in the Browser.
- **Live Deep Search (`/`):** Search across topic titles, deep level 4+ tags, and card text at once to jump straight to exact cards.
- **Calm Topic Inspector:** Click any topic for **Open Gaps**, a non-rescheduling **Cram Deck**, quiet links for weak cards/all cards, expandable **Where missing cards live** breakdowns, and deep subtags.
- **Shared Bridges & Concept Links:** Spot cards shared across multiple subjects or offline TF-IDF concept links between related cards, and trace shortest pathways between any two topics.
- **Anki Colors + Colorblind Mode (`CB`):** Uses Anki's card-state colors (dark green mature, light green young, blue new, yellow suspended) with a one-click Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe mode that updates the map, grid, inspector, and bottom legend together.

**Privacy & safety**
- **100% offline:** Zero external servers, CDNs, or API calls.
- **Read-only on your cards:** Never edits notes, tags, or review history. **Cram Deck** creates a separate non-rescheduling filtered deck (`resched=False`).
- **Open Anki Atlas** from the top toolbar (**Atlas**), **Tools → Anki Atlas**, or `Ctrl+Shift+M` (`Cmd+Shift+M` on Mac).
